# Biology with Mummy: storyboard

Every scene follows the same five beats: **watch** (animation with narration), **watch** (the idea explained), **predict** (guess first), **try it** (hands-on task), **remember** (summary), then a 3 or 4 question quiz. A final round and a cheat sheet finish the explainer.

## Act 1: Photosynthesis and transport in plants

| # | Scene | Big idea | Guess first | Hands-on task |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Plants make their own food | Photosynthesis: the word and symbol equation | Which gas do plants take in for photosynthesis? | Tap an item, then tap the box it belongs in. |
| 1.2 | Chlorophyll and light | Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light, and reflects green | Which colour of light do plants use the least for photosynthesis? | Choose a colour of light, then press Shine. |
| 1.3 | Testing a leaf for starch | Boil, ethanol, rinse, iodine | Why is the leaf boiled in ethanol? | Tap the steps in the right order. |
