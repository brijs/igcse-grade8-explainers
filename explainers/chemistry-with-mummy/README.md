# Chemistry with Mummy

**Live: https://brijs.github.io/igcse-grade8-explainers/explainers/chemistry-with-mummy/**

Grade 8 chemistry in 17 scenes and three acts, plus a 15-question final round and a cheat sheet. Open `index.html`.

- **Act 1, atoms and the periodic table (Unit 4):** protons, neutrons and electrons; atomic and mass number; electron shells; groups and periods; Group 1 alkali metals; Group 7 halogens and Group 0 noble gases.
- **Act 2, bonding and structure of materials (Unit 5):** why atoms bond; ionic bonding; ionic lattices and conductivity; covalent bonding; simple molecules; giant covalent structures (diamond, graphite, silicon dioxide); deciding the structure from properties.
- **Act 3, displacement reactions (Unit 6):** the reactivity series; metal displacement; word and symbol equations; halogen displacement.

`source/` holds the scenes, narration (`script.py`), audio clips and a `STORYBOARD.md`. Rebuild with `python3 ../_shared/build.py chemistry-with-mummy` (see the main README).
