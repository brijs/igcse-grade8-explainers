# Computer Science with Mummy

**Live: https://brijs.github.io/igcse-grade8-explainers/explainers/computer-science-with-mummy/**

Grade 8 computer science in 21 scenes and four acts, plus a 16-question final round and a cheat sheet. Open `index.html`.

- **Act 1, digital citizenship and security (Unit 1):** digital footprint; behaving well online; data privacy; network threats and phishing; security measures; strong passwords and two-step login.
- **Act 2, spreadsheets (Unit 2):** formulas and functions; relative and absolute references; IF and COUNTIF; conditional formatting and data validation; VLOOKUP; pivot tables; charts. The spreadsheet scenes use a small working spreadsheet with a formula bar.
- **Act 3, logic gates and circuits (Unit 3):** NOT, AND, OR; NAND, NOR, XOR; logic circuits and truth tables; the half adder.
- **Act 4, design thinking and CAD (Unit 4):** the design thinking process; empathise, define and ideate; prototype and test; CAD basics (a Tinkercad-style mock-up).

`source/` holds the scenes, narration (`script.py`), audio clips and a `STORYBOARD.md`. Rebuild with `python3 ../_shared/build.py computer-science-with-mummy` (see the main README).
