# Computer Science with Mummy: storyboard

Every scene follows the same five beats: **watch** (animation with narration), **watch** (the idea explained), **predict** (guess first), **try it** (hands-on task), **remember** (summary), then a 3 or 4 question quiz. A final round and a cheat sheet finish the explainer.

## Act 1: Digital citizenship and security

| # | Scene | Big idea | Guess first | Hands-on task |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Your digital footprint | Active and passive footprints; being a good digital citizen | You post a photo, then delete it one minute later. Can other people still have a copy? | Is it an active or a passive digital footprint? |
| 1.2 | Behaving well online | Cyberbullying, plagiarism, copyright and netiquette | You find a great picture online for your project. What should you do? | Situation 1 of 3 |
| 1.3 | Keeping your data private | Personal data and privacy settings | Which of these is safest to put on a public profile? | Is it safe to share publicly, or should it stay private? |
| 1.4 | Threats to a network | Malware, phishing, hacking and denial of service | An email from an unknown sender says: "Your account will close! Click now!" What is it most likely to be? | Click the suspicious parts of the email. Find at least four. |
| 1.5 | Protecting a network | Firewall, antivirus, updates, encryption and backups | Which security measure checks the traffic between your network and the internet? | Which measure deals with the threat? |
| 1.6 | Strong passwords and two-step login | Long, mixed passwords plus a second proof | Which is the strongest password? | Make up a new password. Do not type a real one: nothing is saved or sent anywhere. |
