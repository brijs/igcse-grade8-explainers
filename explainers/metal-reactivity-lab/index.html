Metal reactivity lab

Which metals push other metals out of a solution? Learn the pattern once, then drill it until it sticks.

The one rule

A more reactive metal displaces a less reactive metal from its solution. Learn this order, top to bottom:

▲ most reactiveleast reactive ▼
Read it like a contest. The metal strip wins if it sits above the metal in the solution. If it sits below or is the same metal, nothing happens.

Pattern tricks

The staircase

Every reaction sits in the top-right triangle of the grid. The diagonal and everything below it is "no reaction".

Top row wins

Aluminium displaces every metal below it. Copper, on the bottom row, displaces nothing.

First column loses

Nothing can displace aluminium from its solution, so that whole column is red.

Same metal, no reaction

Aluminium in aluminium sulphate has nothing to swap, so it is always "no reaction".
