# Physics with Mummy

**Live: https://brijs.github.io/igcse-grade8-explainers/explainers/physics-with-mummy/**

Grade 8 physics: physical quantities, motion graphs (distance-time, speed-time), density, and transfer of thermal energy. 17 scenes in four acts, a 20-question final round and a cheat sheet. Open `index.html`. See `../../README.md` for rebuild steps.

## Cranky Mummy (Tamil)

Opt-in via `source/cranky.json`. Tamil phrases, effects and sounds; toggle in the header. Engine and generic phrases: `../_shared/cranky/` (see the Biology README).
